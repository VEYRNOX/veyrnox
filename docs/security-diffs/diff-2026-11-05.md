### src/sign-gate/presignGateOrReject.js (new, 96 lines) — SAFE
**Commit:** 117fccab fix(approvals): honest mainnet revoke copy and unified pre-sign gate (#2942)
**Change summary:** A fail-closed adapter that fetches a fresh RASP artifact and applies the pure `presignGate()` decision. Callers must check `proceedAllowed === true`.
**Assessment:** Read in full. Every non-success path returns BLOCKED: a missing artifact, an `error` field, a non-string `tier`, a thrown probe, a missing/non-array `blockedActions`, and a `blockedActions` that lists `'sign'`. The module does not touch key material and has no egress of its own. It reuses `getFreshRaspArtifact`, which checks `isDeniabilityOrDemoActive()` inside the attestation probe (`attestation.js:244`). Distinct from the older `presignGateOrReject(txLevel)` local to `WalletConnectProvider.jsx`; the two names now exist in the codebase.
